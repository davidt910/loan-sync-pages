# Privacy Policy This application is a personal automation tool used to synchronize a specific recurring bank payment with a private Google Sheets spreadsheet. The script runs locally on the user's computer. Bank credentials are stored locally and are used to access the bank through the configured bank-scraping library. The script filters transactions for the configured payment and sends matching transaction details to the user's Google Sheets spreadsheet. Google OAuth credentials are stored locally to authorize access to Google Sheets. The tool is intended for personal use and is not offered to the public. No independent analytics or advertising services are intentionally integrated into the script. For questions about this tool, contact the repository owner through GitHub.
